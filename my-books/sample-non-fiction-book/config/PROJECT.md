## Concept

**Logline**: A tour through the science of how humans decide — from snap judgments to life-changing choices — revealing the hidden forces that shape every decision we make.

**Synopsis**: Every day we make thousands of decisions, from trivial (what to eat for lunch) to consequential (whether to change careers). Yet most of us have never examined how our minds actually arrive at these choices. Drawing on decades of research in cognitive psychology, behavioral economics, and neuroscience, this book explores the mental machinery behind human decision-making. It reveals the systematic biases that distort our thinking, the emotional undercurrents that steer our choices, and the environmental factors that nudge us in directions we rarely notice. Each chapter pairs scientific findings with real-world stories and concludes with practical strategies readers can apply immediately.

**Key Themes**:
1. The dual-process mind — how fast intuition and slow deliberation interact and sometimes conflict
2. Cognitive biases — the predictable errors wired into human judgment
3. Practical rationality — concrete strategies for making better decisions in everyday life
